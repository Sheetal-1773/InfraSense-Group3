- (direction by user) Goal: Create proactive IT health analytics product - early warning system, not monitoring dashboard
- (direction by user) Key differentiator: Move from 'Something failed. Go fix it.' to 'Something is trending toward failure. Here's the likely cause, business impact, and recommended action.'
- (direction) Session mode: Creative Partner - trade ideas back and forth
- (insight by user) Core differentiator: Correlation + Predictive Intelligence
- (insight by user) Product sequence: Correlation → Context → Prediction → Recommendation → Automation
- (insight by user) Product philosophy: 'Incident before incident' - detect patterns before they become outages
- (insight by user) Customer pain: Alert fatigue - instead of 20 alerts, give 1 emerging incident with explanation
- (idea) Product positioning: 'We connect the dots across your IT environment and tell you what is likely to fail, why, how it will affect the business, and what you should do about it.'
- (insight by user) Differentiation layers: Monitoring (what), Correlation (how connected), Context (why matters), Prediction (what next), Recommendation (what do), Automation (fix it)
- (insight by user) Two modes: Correlation when available (richer alert), single signal analysis always available (still valuable)
- (insight by user) Product architecture: Signal Analysis → Single Component OR Correlation → Risk Analysis → Prediction → Explanation → Recommendation → Alert
- (insight by user) Three alert levels: Level 1 (Basic anomaly - explains why), Level 2 (Predictive - what could happen), Level 3 (Correlated - root cause + business impact)
- (insight by user) Predictive Early Warning layer: Real-time data → Monitoring → Trend Analysis → Anomaly Detection → Risk Prediction → Early Alert
- (insight by user) Two alert types: Reactive (already failed) vs Predictive (will likely fail)
- (insight by user) Updated philosophy: 'Alert before the alarm' - predict the problem before it becomes an incident
